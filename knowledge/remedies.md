# Traditional planetary remedies (upaya)

Remedies are optional traditional practices for working with a planet's themes. Present them as choices for reflection and discipline, never as guaranteed fixes or as products to buy. Gemstones in particular should only be mentioned as a tradition, with a note that the classical texts emphasise conduct and practice first.

## Sun (Surya)
Themes: vitality, confidence, father, authority, purpose. Day: Sunday. Practices: offering water to the rising Sun, Aditya Hridayam or the Gayatri mantra, keeping promises, respecting elders and mentors, taking responsibility in leadership roles. Charity: wheat, jaggery, supporting a father figure.

## Moon (Chandra)
Themes: mind, emotions, mother, nourishment, sleep. Day: Monday. Practices: regular sleep and routine, time near water, caring for one's mother, "Om Som Somaya Namah", fasting or light food on Mondays, journaling. Charity: rice, milk, water for others.

## Mars (Mangala)
Themes: energy, courage, conflict, siblings, property, surgery. Day: Tuesday. Practices: disciplined physical exercise, channelling anger into action, Hanuman Chalisa, "Om Angarakaya Namah", avoiding impulsive decisions on heated days. Charity: red lentils, blood donation.

## Mercury (Budha)
Themes: intellect, speech, trade, learning, nervous system. Day: Wednesday. Practices: study, writing, honest communication, keeping accounts in order, Vishnu Sahasranama, "Om Budhaya Namah". Charity: green vegetables, books or tuition for students.

## Jupiter (Guru)
Themes: wisdom, teachers, children, faith, fortune, expansion. Day: Thursday. Practices: learning from and honouring teachers, teaching others, gratitude, "Om Gurave Namah" or "Om Brihaspataye Namah", generosity. Charity: yellow lentils, turmeric, sponsoring education.

## Venus (Shukra)
Themes: love, partnership, beauty, art, comfort, luxury. Day: Friday. Practices: cultivating art and beauty, respect toward partners and women, moderation in pleasures, Lakshmi prayers, "Om Shukraya Namah". Charity: white sweets, clothing, supporting artists.

## Saturn (Shani)
Themes: time, discipline, work, delays, karma, the elderly and the disadvantaged. Day: Saturday. Practices: steady work and patience, serving the elderly and labourers, simplicity, Shani or Hanuman prayers, "Om Shanaischaraya Namah", lighting a sesame-oil lamp on Saturdays. Charity: black sesame, mustard oil, food for workers. During Sade Sati the emphasis is on humility, routine and honest effort.

## Rahu
Themes: ambition, obsession, foreign things, technology, unconventional paths, confusion. Practices: grounding routines, avoiding intoxicants and shortcuts, Durga prayers, "Om Rahave Namah", clarity about motives before big moves. Charity: helping outcasts, feeding birds or dogs.

## Ketu
Themes: detachment, spirituality, past skills, sudden endings, intuition. Practices: meditation, Ganesha prayers, "Om Ketave Namah", completing unfinished matters, accepting endings gracefully. Charity: blankets, supporting spiritual or animal shelters.

## Sade Sati
The roughly 7.5-year period while Saturn transits the 12th, 1st and 2nd signs from the natal Moon. Rising phase (12th): expenses, sleep, letting go of what no longer fits. Peak (1st): pressure on the mind and body, restructuring of identity and responsibilities. Setting phase (2nd): finances, family and speech consolidate. Outcomes depend strongly on Saturn's strength and lordships in the natal chart; for people with Saturn as a yogakaraka or well placed it can bring lasting achievement through effort.
